The Intelligence Era

08 MIN READ

ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

AI is moving from answering questions to becoming an active partner.

Artificial intelligence is moving into research laboratories, creative studios, hospitals, factories and classrooms. The next stage is not simply about larger models, but about systems that can understand context, use tools and collaborate with people.

The most interesting question is no longer whether machines can process information. It is how humans will redesign their work around intelligent systems.
